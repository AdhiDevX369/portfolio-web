## AI & Systems Projects

### [Rust-Exp: AI Chatbot](https://github.com/AdhiDevX369/rust-exp)
**Stack:** Rust, Ollama
A high-performance, CRUD-based AI chatbot interface. This project leverages Rust’s memory safety and speed to interact with local LLMs via Ollama, providing a private and efficient chat environment.

### [MCP-S2xD: AI vs Human Detector](https://github.com/AdhiDevX369/MCP-S2xD)
**Stack:** Go (Golang)
A specialized tool designed to differentiate between human-generated and AI-generated content. Built with Go for high concurrency and rapid processing speeds.
